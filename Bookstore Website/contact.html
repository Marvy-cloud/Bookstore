Frequently asked questions

A few helpful details before you get in touch.

Can I read books for free on BookHaven?

BookHaven is designed to support free reading content where available. Access to a particular title depends on its content and usage rights. Not every book in a catalogue is automatically free to read.

How do I find a particular book?

Visit the Books page and use the search or category filters, where available, to explore the catalogue.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Order management is still being developed. Once real ordering is enabled, this section should explain the official cancellation and amendment policy.

Does this contact form send messages directly?

Not yet. This version validates the form in your browser. A server-side endpoint or a trusted form service must be connected before messages can reach the BookHaven team.
